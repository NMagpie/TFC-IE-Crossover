New Alloys
Do not get burned!
Since TerraFirmaCraft adds alloy crafting system, now you have possibility to create Immersive Engineering Alloys like Electrum or Constantan in TFC style!<br>
All you have to know are proportions for both alloys (they are pretty easy, trust me).<np>
To create Electrum Alloy you will need to mix 40-60% of Molten Gold and 40-60% of Molten Silver.<br>
Same story is about Constantan, which consists of 40-60% of Molten Copper and 40-60% of Molten Nickel.

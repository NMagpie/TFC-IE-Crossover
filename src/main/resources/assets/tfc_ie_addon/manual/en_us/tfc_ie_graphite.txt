Highly Ordered Pyrolytic Graphite (HOP) is a complex, highly compressed, carbon material used in special engineering constructs.<br>
HOP Graphite Dust is created by compressing eight pieces of Coke Dust or Graphite Powder in the <link;squeezer;Industrial Squeezer>.<br>
